## Do's and don'ts

- Give the logo a strong location.

![[UI_Design_1.png]]

- Provide a hierarchy.

![[UI_Design_2.png]]

- Don't use a placeholder as a label.
- The primary call to action (CTA) should always stand out.

![[UI_Design_3.png]]

## Forms

1. Don't show errors while the user is typing, nor on every keystroke. Validate on blur (when the user leaves the input) or on submit.
2. Don't only flag a wrong field (red). Flag the right ones too (green).
